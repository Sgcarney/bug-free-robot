Privacy Policy

PRIVACY, AFFILIATE MARKETING & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY POLICY PAGE

Mailing list

Any information you provide will never be sold or shared with anyone. We typically issue 24 newsletters a year, including announcements for new books and sales.

Customers in the EU: we comply with all GDPR standards. Please contact admin@scottcarney.com with any questions or concerns. We take your rights seriously.

Affiliate Links

When you click on links to Amazon, Apple, Barnes and Noble, Kobo and Book Depository, you are clicking on an affiliate link. Those links use a tracking cookie to pay the author a small percentage of the proceeds of your purchase. As an affiliated associate of these retailers, we are compensated for purchases you make after clicking through from this site.

From time to time we may add affiliate programs for other products, too.

Facebook

If you are logged onto Facebook when you visit this site, a tracking pixel generated by Facebook may remember you as a visitor to ScottCarney.com, and you may see ads for Scott's books on Facebook from time to time. Facebook stores this connective information and never shares your name or any of your data with us. We do not have access to your Facebook data in any capacity.

Website Purchases

If you buy signed copies or e-books directly from ScottCarney.com, we will see your billing and shipping addresses and the name you provide. But we will not have access to your full credit card number. Payments are handled by a worldwide web transaction provider called Stripe or by Paypal.

Piracy and other forms of Intellectual Property Theft

We do not tolerate internet piracy of any Scott Carney titles. You may receive a legal notice from our lawyers if you participate in willful infringement. Be advised that we collect evidence of infringement before sending our legal notices.
